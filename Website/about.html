🌟 About SpotterAI

Your personal AI-powered fitness companion

🎯 Our Mission

SpotterAI is created to make fitness more accessible, personalized, and motivating. Whether you're lifting weights, tracking calories, or learning new exercises, SpotterAI gives you real-time guidance powered by AI.

💪 What We Offer

• Personalized workout recommendations
• AI-powered chat support
• Media analysis for form checks
• Saved workouts and meal plans
• A clean, simple user experience

👥 Our Team

SpotterAI is built by a dedicated team of CS undergraduates passionate about fitness and technology. Our goal is to help users stay consistent, informed, and confident in their fitness journey.
